How the math works & what the checks mean

The curves

The gold curve is the degree-n Taylor polynomial Tn(x) = ∑ f(k)(a)/k! · (x − a)k, built from exact closed-form derivatives (no numerical differentiation). Factorials up to 16! are exact in JavaScript numbers. Between whole degrees, the animation blends Tn + s·cn+1(x − a)n+1; those in-between curves are a visual transition, not Taylor polynomials.

What each check does

  1. Starting values. c0 equals f(a), and c1 matches a numerical slope of f computed directly from f, not from the derivative formulas.
  2. Differential equation. Each function solves a simple equation (sin, cos: f″ = −f; ex: f′ = f; ln(1+x): (1+x)f″ + f′ = 0; 1/(1−x): (1−x)f′ = f). That equation forces a recurrence between neighbouring coefficients. Checks 1 and 2 together pin down every coefficient uniquely, so a wrong sign or missing factorial fails here.
  3. Error bound at 100 points. Taylor's theorem (Lagrange form) says |f(x) − Tn(x)| ≤ max|f(n+1)| · |x − a|n+1/(n+1)!. The app checks the actual error against this bound across the convergence zone.
  4. Probe. The same comparison at the point x0 you choose.

What the checks don't prove

They're computer checks at sample points, done in 64-bit floating point. They catch mistakes in the formulas and the code, but they aren't a written proof. The proof is Taylor's theorem itself; the checks confirm this program follows it.

The amber "bound too loose" zone

For ln(1+x) and 1/(1−x), part of the convergence interval is hatched amber. There the Lagrange bound is still true, but it grows as n increases, so it can't show the series converges. The series does converge there (watch the actual error shrink); proving it needs another tool, such as the geometric series or the integral form of the remainder. At a = 0 that zone is −1 < x ≤ −½ for ln(1+x), and ½ ≤ x < 1 for 1/(1−x).

Endpoints

ln(1+x) converges at the right endpoint (alternating harmonic series) and diverges at x = −1. 1/(1−x) diverges at both endpoints. sin, cos and ex converge for every x.
